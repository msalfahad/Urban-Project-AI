Structural completeness, cantilever bands and beam conventions

A dated correction layer over S9 (frozen at commit 5f317d2). It re-checks every released quantity, finds the eight hatched bands in the drawing, compares the two readings of the beam depth and separates interpretation questions from missing information. It releases nothing and changes no frozen stage.

Released, unchanged: 158.070 m3 concrete and 18,845.961 kg reinforcement, S9's partial structural BOQ. S9A delta 0.000 m3 / 0.000 kg. The project gate stays INCOMPLETE.

Key findings

  • S9's release holds: 21 of 21 families agree with an independent recompute; the only other difference is 0.001 kg of rounding in the S9 README table.
  • The 8 hatched bands (4.947 m2) are cantilever portions. 7 move from 'untagged beam' to cantilever portion and stay blocked (no thickness); one is beam CA's own hatch.
  • Beam concrete is 56.767 or 70.932 m3 depending on what H means: one engineer answer (RFI-A01) settles it. Nothing is released until then.
  • Slab panels stop at drawn beam and column faces (243 of 243 edges), so neither beam reading counts slab concrete twice.
  • Ties and links: 2,616.648 kg wait for a bend radius and hook (RFI-A02); no source prints one.

How to read the figures

  • SOURCE-DERIVED drawn where the source puts it: DXF entities, S1 polygons, register coordinates
  • SCHEMATIC explains a rule or a reading; not a drawn position
  • CONDITIONAL holds only under an unconfirmed reading; never released, never in a total
