## 4. Rule, tests, lessons

Rule (any villa): a strip is classed by what it joins before it is summed (interior dry: floor; lift / exterior: ONE
threshold line HELD, area kept); an edge line with no opening is no strip: its area goes, part by part, to where it
lies (a named missing piece of another line, a wall top, a wet zone, a held zone), every part placed once; a floor
piece bound WET by a decision moves to the wet line with its area unchanged, a PART of a face only with the closure of
its printed box (every other piece named, residual within 0.001 m2, never absorbed). Code: `engine/source/arch_v3013.py`
kinds TO_WET / NO_OPENING_SPLIT (checks `_check_record`, `_check_counted_in`, `scope_changes`, `check_floor_scope`,
