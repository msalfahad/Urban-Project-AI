## 3. Rule, tests, lessons

Rule (generalised, any villa): a floor-line piece is typed by its physical class before it is summed: an enclosed
interior face enters the internal line (material kept apart), a threshold-class strip leaves it for ONE threshold line
with its area kept (HELD, never 0), an exterior face bound by a decision leaves the internal key for ONE outdoor line
(the difference to the drawn area named), a strip with no drawn opening is no floor. Tests `tests/t240_floor_scope`
(35): villa K-240 hand fixture (dressing 2.10 x 1.40, lift landing 1.10 x 0.20, exterior sill 0.90 x 0.15 with a threshold
twin replaced in place, passage 1.30 x 5.00 with a 0.140 missing band, wall top + twin; internal line 30.915 ->
32.940, +2.025); adverse: shaft class, unsettled wet / dry, material without source, material borrowed from the
neighbour (D-C3), area off the print, millimetres as metres, zero side, held threshold released, threshold at 0, sill in
floor AND threshold, unbound exterior, missing piece unnamed or silent, line named twice, carried figure mismatch, line
not found, earlier change unnamed, outdoor line in the internal key, declared total mismatch, untouched duplicate named;
plus BOQ_FAMILY_MASTER 1.8.0 (1.7.0 + pending V3.0.13) and its registry tests.
