## The 16 cm default and the 18 cm panels

**The 16 cm default is printed, but no person has read it yet.** Panels without their own tag take 16 cm, which is printed twice:

- the p.8 general note N18;
- p.1 note A.

Both are Arabic vector glyphs, read by machine (S1 census, M4 OCR). The freelancer's own label 'S.S 16 CM' uses the same value, but agreement between machine readings is not human verification.

**Sensitivity:** each 1 cm on the default panels is 3.25 m3 (86 KWD), RFI-M5-16.

**The two 18 cm panels** (SITE-33c2a6eeca9700cc 8.235 m2, SITE-3ddef6eb3b8c73b0 5.400 m2) carry a printed 'T 18' tag. They are the water-tank roof (S8.4 measures the same two panels: decision D-01, counted once).

## Faces that are not slab

The 1F face M4 held as a stair zone (17.13 m2, 2.74 m3 exposure) contains the dome-B centre. The dome's radial lines were read as winder treads. S8.3 records no slab inside the dome circle, so the exposure is dropped (D-03). The dome-A interior is a beam-strip face and was never counted.
