Unresolved concrete - numbered locations

red: section or label unresolved; amber: lintel need unresolved (beam over the opening). The number is the NO column of the exception register.

Continuous beams - spans and supports

pieces by span-matching state (green forward, blue reversed / inherited by the printed span sequence, amber ambiguous direction, red count or length conflict); labels are mark.span

Exterior ground beams

blue: exterior wall evidenced (exterior depth); amber: exterior wall but the inside level gives no deeper section (class section, conflict); orange: perimeter without a wall; red: no architectural registration; the printed inside level is shown

Slab thickness basis

green-mint: project default; teal: printed in the panel; pale teal: sheet-uniform; orange: no thickness; pink outline: member overlap deducted; numbers in cm

Blinding footprints

orange: footing blinding (footing + projection); blue: ground / strap beam strips registered onto the foundation plan; their overlap is deducted once

Lintels

green: measured (no beam over the opening); amber: a beam covers the opening (need unresolved); red: outside the schedule

Stair zones

drawn tread lines inside each stair zone

Missing reinforcement

counts and the concrete left without bar data - never kg by ratio
