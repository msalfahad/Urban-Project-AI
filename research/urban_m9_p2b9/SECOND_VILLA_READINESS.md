The order follows the sealed protocol.

| # | Item | Why |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **One complete second villa set**, never shown to the engine: every plan level, **sections through every floor** (F.F.L., F.C.L., U/S SLAB), **elevations**, the **door and window schedule** (mark, width, height, sill), the finishes schedule or room data sheet, and the legend | V2.8 reads heights only from drawings; a set without sections and schedules gives held wall areas, as the reference plan does |

| 3 | The QS's take-off of the protocol metrics, with the conventions stated wherever an owner rule is still open (D-16 door minimum, D-17 review spaces, kitchen membrane, skirting under windows, reveal finish) | the scorer compares like with like |

## 4. What the result could and could not show

- **One blind villa** gives coverage and per-category error on that villa. It cannot support an accuracy percentage:
  the claim rule needs two independent blind projects.
- **PARTIAL categories** (heights, windows, reveals) will show their gaps. A failure there is expected until the
  drawings carry the evidence.
