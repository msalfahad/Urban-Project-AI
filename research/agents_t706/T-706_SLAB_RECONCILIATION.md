## 1. Physical element (ERS s.2.2)

A suspended slab panel is the cast-in-situ concrete between member faces (beams, columns, the ring beams) at its own
thickness, net of drawn voids (X symbol), dome openings (radial symbol), stair zones (flights are the stair family) and
member strips (beam concrete is billed full depth by the beam, R-CONC-OWN-1 / T-545: slab NET of the beam plan). The
thickness is the mark printed inside the panel ('T 18'), else the sheet's 0.16. The owner manual method (panel by
panel, net between member faces, printed thickness) and the drawing-derived method (own noding of the member and slab
edge lines) are the same measurement here; the reference uses the same convention on GF (s.3), not gross areas (the
beam plan is 60.3 / 42.2 / 9.7 m2 per storey, far beyond any residual).
