How a record becomes a line: RELEASED -> SOURCE_DERIVED kg, evidence DRAWING-CONFIRMED (VERIFIED only where a checker run
re-measured that row), NOT RELEASED; HELD_RANGE -> BOUNDED_EXPOSURE low .. high (min .. max of the ledger's readings);
HELD_DEFINED -> BLOCKED_RFI, the defined kg only in its inputs (never added); HELD_LOWER_BOUND -> BLOCKED_RFI, lower
bound in its inputs, upper NOT_ESTABLISHED; UNQUANTIFIED -> BLOCKED_RFI, UNKNOWN. A RELEASED record whose count does not
come from a printed schedule row of its own (DUPLICATE_ROW / NOT_PRINTED / missing) is forced to UNKNOWN, never
released or verified, whatever else it claims (fixture: planted column with a duplicated row, 41.718519 kg kept as
information). Route G's provisional steel lines of each ledger's scope (physical element + storeys) are SUPERSEDED
(NOT_APPLICABLE_WITH_EVIDENCE, route kg in the note); the 10 steel lines of the 5 undrawn columns (V3.0.1 NOT_PRESENT)
are superseded with that reason and leave with their concrete. Necks, slabs, ground slab and footings are outside every
scope and byte-identical.
