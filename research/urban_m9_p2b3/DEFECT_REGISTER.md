# Defect register and fixes (deliverable 8)

Every defect below was found by a proof comparison failing, or by a check written for the proof library. Each was
reproduced before the fix: the frozen V2.4 result stays in the comparison as FAILED_VALIDATION
(`test_v2_4_fails_exactly_where_the_proof_found_its_defects`, `test_v2_4_differs_only_at_the_reach_boundary`). The
fixes live in the opt-in method ARCH_TAKEOFF_V2.5 only; V1 .. V2.4 are byte-identical (REGRESSION_AND_FROZEN_INTEGRITY.md).
