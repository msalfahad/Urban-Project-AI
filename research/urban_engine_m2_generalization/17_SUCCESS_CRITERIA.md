# The ten questions of the brief (Phase 19), answered plainly

| # | question | answer | evidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Can a completely new villa be processed? | **Yes, for DXF structural sets drawn in the conventions tested; not yet proven on a second real villa.** The one command runs end to end on 170 unseen synthetic drawings (70 clean, 60 defect, 40 stress) and on Alsenan with no frozen register. DWG and raster-only PDFs are refused or reported. | 11, 04 |
| 2 | Can the engine detect its beams and slabs? | **Yes, on synthetic: recall and precision 1.0 for beams and panels, including curved beams, untagged spans, width conflicts, openings and drafting noise. On Alsenan, beam concrete is within 2 % of the frozen replay and slab within 5 %.** 15 unbound bands, 7 unbound tags and one open-boundary face are reported. | 11, 04, overlays |
| 3 | Can it calculate meaningful concrete quantities? | **Yes for footings, columns, beams, ground beams, slabs and the slab-on-grade, under both beam-depth readings.** In-scope Alsenan concrete is 0.92 of the frozen replay, with every gap evidenced. **No** for stairs, lifts, pools, domes, tanks, lintels, blinding and cantilevers: these are not quantified, and are listed in BOQ section C. | 04 |
| 4 | Can it calculate reinforcement where bar details are available? | **Yes where the bars are in schedules or readable notes**, as bar models (diameter-aware hooks, closed links, laps and anchorage as named conventions), never a kg/m³ ratio. **No** where the bars are only in a vector-glyph PDF (Alsenan ground beams), or per span on continuous beams: these are MISSING_DATA, never zero. | 05 |
| 5 | Can it identify missing or uncertain quantities? | **Yes, mostly.** UNRESOLVED items carry exposure ranges. The coverage-risk register covers unbound marks and bands, footings without outlines, schedule rows not seen on plan, open slab faces, unenclosed band sides, widened snaps, duplicate marks and out-of-scope families with their evidence or "no label found – confirm absent". **Limit:** a family with no label and no detector still shows only as "confirm absent". | 04, 07, 11 |
| 6 | Does it prevent double counting? | **Yes, by construction and by test.** Columns own their footprint (bands split at supports), slab faces stop at band faces, beam junctions go to one owner, nested footings count once. On synthetic drawings the BEAM, COLUMN and PANEL face areas equal the design owner areas exactly, and no band overlaps a column or panel. | `test_synthetic_villas` |
| 7 | Does it explain every assumption? | **Yes.** 20 named generic conventions plus profile values, each with base, range, provenance and rationale, sit in ASSUMPTIONS and in every item's assumption list. Sensitivity shows one change at a time. **But:** the conventions are engineering placeholders (PROVISIONAL_CODE_METHOD), not approvals. | `alsenan_route_g/ASSUMPTIONS.csv`, `SENSITIVITY.csv` |
| 8 | Does it generate a usable BOQ? | **Yes, as a planning estimate.** It produces an Excel workbook (concrete, plain concrete, reinforcement by diameter, by floor, the V / P / U registers, not detected, coverage risk, rebar completeness, assumptions, sensitivity, exceptions), a PDF report and source-coordinate overlays. It has no prices. **It is not for tender or contract:** no quantity of a new project is VERIFIED. | `alsenan_route_g/`, `synthetic_demo_villa_7/` |
| 9 | How accurate is it against independent ground truth? | **On synthetic design truth: within 1e-6 for every family, on every held-out drawing.** That truth shares the generator's drafting assumptions. **On real drawings: unknown.** The only real comparison is with our own frozen replay (in-scope concrete 0.92, steel 0.86). The 95 % target is **not** demonstrated. | 11, 12 |
| 10 | How much manual work is still required? | **For a new villa:** a profile with printed storey heights (2–4 values), a review of the coverage-risk register, and answers to RFIs where bars or sections are not readable. On Alsenan that means 4 profile inputs and 9 RFIs. **Out-of-scope families need manual quantities**, or the frozen stages for Alsenan. | 14, profile_st7757.json |
