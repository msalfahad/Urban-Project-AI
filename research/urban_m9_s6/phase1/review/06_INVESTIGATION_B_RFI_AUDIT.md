## Findings this audit raises (new)

1. **S6R-B1: drawn doors silently missing as elements.**
   - Door swings drawn: GF 16, 1F 10, 2F 3 (29 in all).
   - 21 are matched to band openings; one double-leaf pair counts as a single door, giving 20 `ARCH.DOOR` lines.
   - 7 are state `CLOSED`: the swing closes the room boundary, but no opening is measured.
   - 1 is `OPENING_CLOSURE_UNRESOLVED`, a warning only.
   - So **8 drawn doors have no door line, in V1 and in V2**.
   - Phase 1's completeness gate checks *surfaces*, not *elements*, so it did not catch this.
   - Five of these doors surface only as "threshold finish" RFIs, which asks the owner a question about a door the engine never counted.
2. **S6R-B2: engine defects are emitted as client RFIs.** ENG-* lines (14) should be REVIEW_REQUIRED engineering findings with a SOFTWARE cause, never client questions. A client cannot answer "your strip failed OUTSIDE_OPENING_BOX".
3. **S6R-B3: one RFI line per element instead of one per question.**
   - The 25 window reveal lines are answered by one window schedule.
   - The 24 threshold lines are answered by one owner rule.
   - The fix (R4 below) is an RFI register keyed by question, which lists the affected elements and the measured quantities.
4. **S6R-B4: a rule-authority conflict behind Q7.**
   - `URBAN-WET-WALL-TILE-FULL-HEIGHT@v1` (method register; used by `finish_height.py`) says wet-room tile runs to the finished ceiling, so there is no band.
   - Owner-approved `UP-CER-009` (1.5.0; used by `arch_measure.heights`) says tile height is derived per face and never a constant.
   - The two engine paths disagree. **If the owner confirms full-height tile in wet rooms, all 16 Q7 lines close as NOT_APPLICABLE.**
5. **S6R-B5: no plaster or paint quantity exists on the reference plan in V1 or V2.**
   - All 30 plaster and 30 paint lines are BLOCKED_RFI, because the finish heights are not established.
   - The coverage gate says PASS because every surface ends in a line or an RFI.
   - That is accountability, not quantification (Investigation C).

## Answer to "increasing the number of RFIs is not automatically improving the engine"

Agreed, and this audit is the evidence.

The improvement Phase 1 actually made: 85 surfaces that were silently absent are now visible, each with its measured geometry. That is real, because a silent omission is the most expensive error in a BOQ.

But the right way to show it is a different set of metrics:
- **"surfaces silently absent: 85 → 0"**;
- **"distinct questions: 4"**;
- **"engine defects found: 6 groups / 14 lines"**.

"RFI lines: 123 → 208" is the wrong metric. Phase 2 should report these three counts and move the ENG-* items out of the client RFI register (S6R-B2, S6R-B3).
