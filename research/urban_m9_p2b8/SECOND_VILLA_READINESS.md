## 3. The independent reference method

1. **Author.** An independent quantity surveyor appointed by the owner, never shown any engine output for this villa.
2. **Method.** `QS_HAND_TAKEOFF` or `QS_CAD_MEASUREMENT`, measured from the issued drawings, with the sha256 of every file used.
3. **What is measured.** Per room: floor, wet floor, wall ceramic, ceiling, double-height exclusion, plaster, paint, skirting and waterproofing (floor, upturn, doorway). Per door: opening width, class and leaf. Per window: width. Per floor: wall run and the counts of openings, windows and doors by class. Per stair: treads, tread area and landing.
4. **Unsupported metrics.** A metric the engine does not support may still be measured: door and window heights, sills, glazing, masonry, risers, sloping flights and rails. It is reported as UNSUPPORTED with its total, never scored.
5. **Matching key.** Each element carries a point inside it in the drawing's coordinates (metres).
6. **Audit.** Before sealing, the custodian may audit the QS figures with `tools/indep_reader.py`. The QS settles every difference; the reader is never the reference.
7. **Conventions.** Wherever an owner rule is still open (D-16, D-07, kitchen waterproofing, skirting under windows), the QS states the convention used.
8. **Commitment.** The custodian publishes sha256(salt + reference) and the protocol sha256 before the run.

## 5. Limits of what the result will be able to say

- **One blind villa.** It can show the engine's coverage and error per category on that villa. It cannot support an accuracy percentage: the V1 claim rule needs two independent blind projects.
- **PARTIAL metrics.** These will show their gaps. A PROPOSED_FAIL on them is the expected, honest outcome until the gaps are closed.
- **Ceilings and wall runs.** They are PROVISIONAL in the engine, so they are scored as provisional answers and never counted as released.
