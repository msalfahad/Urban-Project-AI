## Acceptance record (beside MERGED)

Schema `URBAN_ACCEPTANCE_RECORD_V1`; required: schema, acceptance_id, subject_kind, subject_id, task, candidate_version, state, measurement_proof, checker, time; optional: aggregation_proof (required for VERIFIED / VERIFIED ZERO of a LINE, TRADE or BOQ), merge, author, notes.
Subject kinds LINE, COMPONENT, TRADE, BOQ; states VERIFIED, VERIFIED ZERO, VERIFIED COMPONENT; measurement proofs CHECKER_VERDICT_V2, T180_REMEASURE, ABSENCE_FROM_DRAWINGS; aggregation proofs T469_SCOREBOARD, T471_COMPLETENESS, EXPORT_COMPLETENESS_RECORD.
MERGED (board) is never a proof; the Checker / Verifier alone accepts; the author cannot accept.
