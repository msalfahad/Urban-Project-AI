## 4. General rule (proposed, any villa; new file, never an edit): UP-OPEN-CLOSURE-001 v1.0.0

(a) On an elevation, a window's opening is the outline whose jamb-to-jamb width binds to the plan width within 0.02 m
(and whose spacing reproduces the plan spacing); leaves drawn beside the jambs are the shutter item, a frame drawn inside
is a fabrication size; identical twins transfer values without deciding which is which, differing twins with symmetric
spacing stay AMBIGUOUS; a head is never taken from a neighbouring opening. (b) A glazed run's count comes from the drawn
span: a pier needs a drawn transverse line, an architectural column, or a structural column that continues above the
floor; a member stopping below is no pier; a render with another count holds the line (s.8) without changing the drawn
count. (c) A head soffit is the reveal's head term, owned by the reveal line, never a separate line. (d) A lintel is
established when the lowest member soffit bound clears head + lintel depth. (e) A threshold needs a door, a level change
or an owner rule; a finish change alone is an edge whose item is UNKNOWN.
