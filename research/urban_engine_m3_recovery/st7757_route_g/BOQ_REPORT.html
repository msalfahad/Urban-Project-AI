How to read it. Verified is the only quantity released under the strict rules. Provisional is calculated from drawn or printed geometry with the assumptions listed below; each provisional item carries one reading as its base and the others as its range. The planning estimate is verified + provisional base; its low and high add each item's own low and high (a bracket, not a probability). Unresolved items have no defensible quantity and are never added: 30 concrete items (13.5 to 21.9 m3 indicative exposure on 27 of them) and 28 reinforcement items. The planning estimate is therefore not complete.
