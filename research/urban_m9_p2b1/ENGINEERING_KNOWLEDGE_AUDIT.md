## 4. Consolidation performed

- **Stable rule ids and machine-readable records**: 25 records in `knowledge/URBAN_ENGINEERING_KNOWLEDGE_1.0.0.json`
  (23 OWNER_APPROVED, 1 PROJECT_SPECIFIC, 1 DEPRECATED), each with purpose, scope, elements, locations, exceptions,
  authority (source and date), status, logic, required evidence, permitted assumptions, conflicts, dependent trades,
  code links, test links, examples, limitations and history (`OWNER_RULE_REGISTER.md`).
- **Duplicates and contradictions**: 11 conflicts recorded with their resolution (CF-01 .. CF-11,
  `RULE_CONFLICT_PRECEDENCE_MATRIX.md`); two remain open and are owner questions (CF-03 -> OQ-01, CF-11 -> OQ-02).
- **Outdated interpretations**: UP-DOOR-MIN-001 is DEPRECATED with `superseded_by` UP-DOOR-OPEN-002; the old reading is
  recorded so it cannot return silently (`test_the_superseded_part_c_reading_is_not_applied`).
- **Provenance**: every record cites its authority (owner correction 2026-10-14, master program part and section,
  door rules 2026-10-13, method register record and version) and carries a history entry; `rules/` is unchanged (its
  digest is in V1 .. V2.2's RUN_RULES; `test_the_rule_store_gains_no_file`).
- **Links to code and tests**: every record names the function that applies it and the tests that prove it;
  `test_every_code_and_test_link_exists` fails if a link is dangling. The registry is built by `tools/kb_build.py`
  (`--check` in `test_the_registry_is_the_builder_output`) and indexed by `tools/kb_index.py`.
- **Project-specific knowledge is never general**: PS-REF-001 (the reference project's outdoor service corridor) is
  PROJECT_SPECIFIC and applies only through a project decision bound to one space by floor and point
  (UP-SPACE-002); `test_project_decisions_are_never_general`.
