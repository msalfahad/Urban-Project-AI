## 6. Success criteria and rollback triggers (checked at every check-in)

Success after 24 h, against P0: merged per day at least +40 %; READY at most 6 and oldest at most 6 h; rejection share at
most 25 %; closure at least 95 %; at least 2 more reference-line components checker-verified; at least 1 Gate B package
merged; no regression or freeze failure; 0 queue-check flags.

Rollback (the auditor reverts the cron, logs it here and in OWNER_SESSION_CHANGES.md, and reports): more than 8 READY or
oldest older than 8 h -> Checker hourly stays, workers back to 2 h; rejection share above 35 % over at least 8 verdicts
-> workers back; 2 or more same-agent overlaps (IDLE lock lines) or board push failures -> the agent concerned back;
closure below 85 % -> all back; a regression or freeze failure merged -> all back; an agent idle on more than 40 % of
its runs -> that agent back.
